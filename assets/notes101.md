**Conductor 1.0.1**

Download **Conductor.dmg**, open it and double-click **Install Conductor.pkg** (installs over 1.0.0; settings stay).

- Phone Remote fits any phone screen, in portrait and landscape
- Live meters on the phone: main output and both headphones
- Headphone mixer: stereo-linked pairs, two rows of eight with the output on the right, compact labels, Option-click resets / Command-click takes a fader down
- The mixer return no longer plays in the headphones

- Stream Deck: Conductor for Companion module (dials for levels, keys for mute/dim/mono, speaker sets and headphone mixes), included in the download
