**Conductor 1.0.0**

Download **Conductor.dmg**, open it and double-click **Install Conductor.pkg**. The guide is included in the download and attached here.

- Monitoring: monitor knob, DIM / MUTE, speaker sets up to 7.1, mono, two headphone outputs; menu bar panel
- Headphone Mixes: two zero-latency cue mixes, stereo-linked pairs, one-click DAW rough mix
- Phone Remote: scan the QR code to control monitoring from your phone
- Routing, I/O levels, calibration measurements, profiles
- Restores the interface's settings after a power cycle

Requires macOS 14 or later (Apple silicon or Intel). If macOS won't open the installer, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
